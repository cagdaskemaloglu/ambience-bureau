// INITIAL_NETWORK_SETUP_ROADMAP_
Follow the official protocol below in sequence to integrate the asset 
into the central network:

1. TERMINAL CONNECTION: Connect the device to the main power source (220V). 
   The system will automatically establish a local gateway (Access Point).
2. SIGNAL ACCESS: Navigate to the Wi-Fi settings on your mobile device and 
   connect to the temporary local network broadcasted as "ESP32-Setup".
3. PROTOCOL TRANSFER: Open the official Bureau mobile software. Assign your 
   primary home/office Wi-Fi network name and credentials to the device.
4. SYNCHRONIZATION: Once configuration is complete, reconnect your mobile 
   device to your primary Wi-Fi network. The system is now fully operational.
